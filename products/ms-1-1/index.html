MS 1.1

Market Structure Multi-Timeframe Chart.
See market structure across multiple timeframes without constantly switching charts.

Coming Soon on MQL5 Market Documentation
Core Capabilities

Market structure intelligence, organized in one chart.

MS 1.1 is designed to bring multi-timeframe market structure into a single, controlled workspace.

Instead of constantly switching between charts, traders can monitor the active timeframe together with selected reference timeframes, while keeping important structural information clearly separated and easy to read.

BOL, POI and SM structures, Trend and Range context, configurable display presets, candlestick pattern recognition and market-structure alerts work together to provide a more complete view of how price is developing across multiple timeframes.

Multi-Timeframe Structure Combine the active chart timeframe with selected reference timeframes and monitor their structure from a single chart.
BOL, POI & SM Visualize Break-of-Level, Point-of-Interest and Smart-Money structures through one consistent market-structure framework.
Trend & Range Context Keep directional trend and active range conditions visible alongside the underlying market structure.
Flexible Display Control Choose between Basic, Performance, Standard, Full and Custom presets to control how much structural information is displayed.
Pattern Recognition Advanced candlestick pattern recognition adds directional context and helps highlight relevant price-action setups.
Structure Alerts Receive alerts for supported structural events so important market changes can be identified without continuously monitoring the chart.
Interface overview

Choose the display density that fits the workflow.

MS 1.1 includes five display presets, from a clean overview to a fully manual setup.

Basic Essential BOL lines and zones on the active timeframe, with a clear reference-timeframe view.
Performance A lighter display focused on the information needed for fast chart reading and smoother execution.
Standard A balanced view combining the main structural lines with useful context from selected reference timeframes.
Full The richest predefined view, showing the broadest supported combination of structure, zones and context.
Custom Manual control of BOL, POI and SM lines, zones and executed levels for each selected timeframe.
